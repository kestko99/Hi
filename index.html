⚠️ Crypto Investment Risks

Don't invest unless you're prepared to lose all the money you invest. This is a high-risk investment and you are unlikely to be protected if something goes wrong.

🔴 Key Risks

  • Extreme Volatility: Crypto prices can drop 50%+ in days
  • Total Loss: You could lose 100% of your investment
  • No Protection: Unlike banks, crypto has no government insurance
  • Irreversible: Transactions cannot be reversed if sent to wrong address
  • Scams: The crypto space has many fraudulent projects

💡 Safety Tips

  • Only invest what you can afford to lose
  • Research thoroughly before investing in any cryptocurrency
  • Use secure wallets and enable 2-factor authentication
  • Never share your private keys or seed phrases
  • Start small and learn before making larger investments
  • Be skeptical of promises of guaranteed returns
